RK ALUMINIUM FABRICATION WEBSITE

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or another browser.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Open index.html in a text editor.
2. Replace "Add your business email" and "Add your phone number" with your real business details.
3. In the contact form JavaScript, replace YOUR-EMAIL@example.com with your real email address.
4. Replace the illustrative gallery images in assets/ with photos of your actual completed work.
5. Update the service area to the cities you actually cover.

NOTES
- This is a responsive, static HTML/CSS/JavaScript website; no installation or build tools are required.
- The quote form opens the visitor's default email app. It does not store submissions on a server.
- Images are illustrative design examples, not verified photos of RK Aluminium Fabrication projects.
